# Moods Companion — Support

Thanks for using **Moods Companion**, a scorekeeper and card reference for the
Mood Swings card game.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does the app work offline?**
Yes. Moods Companion works fully offline — it never connects to the internet.

**Is my data private?**
Completely. The app has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. Your games are
saved only on your device. See the Privacy Policy for details.

**How do I keep score?**
On the Game tab, tap the right side of a player's card to add a point and the
left side to subtract. Tap **End Round** to award the round to the top scorer.

**How do teams work?**
In a Team game, each team shares one combined score. Tap the team's card to
adjust it.

**Where do the cards and rules come from?**
The Cards and Rules tabs are a reference for the physical game. The app tracks
scores and rounds — it doesn't enforce card effects.
