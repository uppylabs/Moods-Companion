Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 July 2026

Moods Companion ("the app") is designed to respect your privacy. This policy explains what the app does and does not do with information.

The short version

The app collects no personal data, shows no ads, and makes no network requests. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information we collect

None. The app does not have accounts, sign-in, analytics, advertising, or any third-party SDKs. We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share any data.

Information stored on your device

To keep score between sessions, the app saves your current game — including the player or team names you type and their scores and rounds — locally on your device using the operating system's standard on-device storage. This data:

  • never leaves your device,
  • is not sent to us or anyone else,
  • is not accessible to us, and
  • is removed when you start a new game or delete the app.

Network use

The app does not connect to the internet and functions fully offline.

Children's privacy

Because the app collects no data and requires no account, it does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new effective date.
